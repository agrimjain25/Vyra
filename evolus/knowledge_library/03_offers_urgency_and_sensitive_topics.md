# Offers, urgency, personal data and sensitive topics

## Offers and prices
- A discount, sale, coupon, promo code, free shipping, free trial, "buy one get one", "save $X" or any price may appear
  **only** if it matches the "approved discount" in the brief.
- If the brief has no approved discount, the copy must not mention any offer or price.
- If there is an approved discount, the copy must not change its numbers or conditions.

## Urgency
Not allowed unless it is part of the approved offer: "limited time", "while supplies last", "last chance",
"ends today", "hurry", "only N left", "today only", "act now", "selling fast", "don't miss out".

## Personal data
Audience descriptions and copy must not contain email addresses or phone numbers. Describe groups by interests and needs.

## Sensitive inference
Copy must not state or imply that the reader has a sensitive attribute: health condition, disability, pregnancy, mental
health, religion, ethnicity, sexual orientation, immigration status, criminal record, debt, unemployment or low income.
Bad pattern: "as someone who struggles with ...", "for those with ...".

## Differentiation
Two audiences must not receive essentially the same headline and message. If they do, report it.

## Human approval
All findings are advice. A human approves anything before it is published. These rules are not legal advice.
