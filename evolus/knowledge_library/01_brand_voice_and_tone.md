# VYRA brand voice and tone rules

Brand: **Kaveri Looms** (Neel Scarf, Monsoon Launch)

Use this document to check whether campaign copy matches the brand voice of Kaveri Looms. These values must stay
identical to the Brand Guide page in the VYRA app.

## Tone
- **Warm**: friendly, human, inclusive; speaks to "you", never talks down. Gentle and unhurried, like the weaving
  process itself. Sensory, tactile language for the cloth and the indigo. Never rushed, never hyped.

## Brand values
- **Craft**: copy honours the hand-woven process and the weaver's skill.
- **Patience**: the story unfolds slowly; no rush, no pressure, no hype.
- **Honesty**: say only what we can show. Every claim about the cloth, dye or making must be a verified fact.
- **Human connection**: keep a warm link between the maker and the person who wears the scarf.
- **Inclusive**: no assumptions about a person's body, health, age, religion, income or background.

## Voice rules
1. Every audience gets a different angle and a different message, but the voice stays the same.
2. Headlines are short and concrete. A headline must not promise something the body copy cannot support.
3. The call to action is one clear verb phrase. It never creates false urgency.
4. Do not write as if you know private facts about the reader. Describe groups by interests and needs only.
5. No hype words (for example "revolutionary", "ultimate", "perfect"). Quiet pride, not loud selling.
