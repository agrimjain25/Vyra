# Review checklist and output format

Checklist for every generated experience (headline, message, call to action, story scenes):
1. Does every factual claim appear in the verified facts? (see claims and evidence rules)
2. Does any offer, price or urgency go beyond the approved discount?
3. Does the tone match the brand tone and values?
4. Does the text infer anything sensitive about the reader?
5. Is this audience's message clearly different from the others?

Finding categories used by VYRA: unsupported_claim, voice, misleading_offer, sensitive_inference, differentiation, other.
Severity: error (must fix or approve before publishing) or warning (a human should look).

When unsure, say so and hand the case to a human reviewer instead of guessing.
